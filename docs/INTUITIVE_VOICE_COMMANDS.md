## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Just say the word** - No need to say "pose" before it
   - ✅ "Aria, jump!"
   - ❌ "Aria, do pose jump"

2. **Numbers still work** - If you prefer numbers
   - "Pose 5" or just "5" = shy pose
   - "Pose 12" or just "12" = jump

3. **Multiple aliases** - Many words work for the same pose
   - "wave", "hello", "hi", "greet" → All do the greeting wave

4. **Natural phrasing** - Speak naturally
   - "Can you sit down?" ✅
   - "Sit" ✅
   - "Sitting" ✅

## 🗣️ How It Works

1. **You speak** → Microphone captures your voice
2. **AI understands** → Gemini 2.5 Pro processes your request
3. **Avatar responds** → Performs the pose + speaks back
4. **Lip sync active** → Mouth moves with speech
5. **Body animates** → Natural movements while speaking
