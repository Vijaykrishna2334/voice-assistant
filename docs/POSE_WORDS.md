## Alternative Words (Also Work!)

- **rest**: sit, sitting
- **casual**: stand, standing
- **chill**: relax, relaxing
- **step**: walk, walking
- **cute**: shy, sweet, bashful
- **lean**: leaning, tilt
- **reach**: stretch, stretching
- **move**: dance, dancing
- **twirl**: spin, spinning
- **sway**: rock, rocking
- **groove**: vibe, vibing
- **bounce**: jump, jumping, hop
- **down**: land, landing, crouch
- **hello**: wave, hi, hey, greet
- **love**: heart, hearts, affection
- **model**: pose, posing, photo

## Quick Test Commands

Try saying:
- "Aria, **rest**"
- "Do a **twirl**"
- "Show me **love**"
- "**Bounce** for me"
- "Say **hello**"
- "Strike a **model** pose"
- "**Chill** with me"
- "**Reach** up high"

---

**Numbers still work!** You can also say "Pose 15" or just "15" instead of "love".
