## 🎯 HOW TO USE

1. **Open the app** at http://localhost:3000
2. **Click anywhere** to unlock audio
3. **Allow microphone** access
4. **Say any command** from the lists above
5. **Watch Aria respond** with the pose + voice!

### Tips:
- Speak clearly and naturally
- You can combine with other requests: "Aria, can you jump and then sit?"
- Say multiple poses: "Show me pose 1, then pose 8"
- AI will understand context: "Do that again" will repeat last pose

---

## 🔧 How It Works

1. You speak → Microphone captures
2. Speech converted to text
3. AI (Gemini 2.5 Pro) understands your command
4. AI responds with `[GESTURE: pose5]` tag
5. Avatar performs the pose from your .vroidpose file
6. TTS speaks the response
7. Avatar's mouth syncs with speech!

---

## 📊 Technical Details

- **Total Poses**: 16 individual .vroidpose files
- **Pose Hold Time**: 3 seconds each
- **Animation Sequences**:
  - Jump: 0.8s (2 poses)
  - Dance: 2.4s (4 poses, looping)
  - Walk: 1.6s (4 poses, looping)
- **Lip Sync**: Real-time mouth movement
- **Facial Expressions**: Happy, sad, excited, loving, thoughtful, neutral
